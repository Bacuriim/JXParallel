# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the JXParallel community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent
- collaborate with empathy
- keep technical discussions fact-based

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project community.
